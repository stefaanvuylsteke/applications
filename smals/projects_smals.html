Proximus — Billing Service
Business & Service Designer · Belgium · 2024
Proximus wanted to reduce late payments, repeated reminders and softcut risk across its billing service. I analysed the payment journey, mapped behavioral barriers and clarified where customers lost attention, confidence or urgency. Based on this, I redesigned the service logic around clearer timeframes, better intervention moments and more supportive communication. The work resulted in nudged email concepts and two complementary flows: one to help customers complete their monthly payment on time, and one to build better long-term payment habits before problems escalated. This gave the team a clearer way to connect billing moments, customer behaviour and service interventions, and to move from repeated reminders to more preventive support.
